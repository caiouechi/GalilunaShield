# Galiluna Shield — Acceptable Use Policy

**Version 1.0 — effective 2026-09-23**

> **TEMPLATE FOR LEGAL REVIEW.** Not legal advice. This policy forms part of the EULA.

Galiluna Shield is built for **one purpose**: to help a parent or legal guardian protect a **minor child**
in their care on a computer the adult owns or administers. Using it for anything else is a breach of the
EULA and may be a criminal offence.

## You MAY use Galiluna Shield to

- monitor a computer **you own or administer** that is used by **your own minor child** or a minor for
  whom you are the legal guardian;
- do so to protect that child from harm (grooming, predators, self-harm, bullying, and the other risks the
  Software looks for);
- keep records and evidence for the purpose of protecting that child, and share them with school
  authorities, counsellors, or the police where appropriate.

## You MUST NOT use Galiluna Shield to

- monitor **any adult** (a partner, ex-partner, housemate, employee, relative) without a clear, lawful
  basis and any consent the law requires — covert surveillance of an adult is often a **crime**;
- monitor a person or a device you have **no legal authority** over;
- **stalk, harass, control, or intimidate** anyone, including in the context of domestic or intimate-
  partner relationships;
- record other people (for example the other side of a call) where your local law forbids recording
  without their consent;
- capture, keep, or share material in a way that breaks the law;
- resell, redistribute, or repackage the Software or its captured data.

## Your legal duties as the operator

Because you control all the data and the recording happens on your device, **you** are responsible for:

1. **Authority.** Confirming you are the parent/guardian of the monitored child (or otherwise lawfully
   authorised) and that you own/administer the computer.
2. **Recording law.** Complying with the recording and wiretapping laws where you and the monitored person
   live. Some places require **all parties** to a conversation to consent before it may be recorded. If
   you cannot meet that requirement, use **microphone-only** mode or turn recording **off**, and rely on
   live alerts instead of stored recordings.
3. **Third parties.** Being aware that system-audio recording can capture other children's voices, and
   only doing so where lawful.
4. **Data protection.** Where laws like the GDPR/UK GDPR/CCPA/COPPA apply, meeting your obligations as the
   controller of the child's (and any third party's) personal data: keep it secure, keep it no longer than
   needed, use it only to protect the child, and honour the rights of the people whose data it is.
5. **Proportionality and the child's wellbeing.** Using the least intrusive settings that meet your goal.
   Child-safety experts widely recommend talking to your child about online safety and, where appropriate,
   about monitoring, rather than relying on secret surveillance alone.

## Not an emergency service

Galiluna Shield does not contact anyone for you and is not monitored by us. If a child is in immediate
danger, contact your local emergency services and the appropriate child-protection authorities.
