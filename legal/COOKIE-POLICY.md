## 1. The desktop Software uses no cookies

Galiluna Shield is a desktop application, not a website. **The Software itself does not use cookies or any
similar browser-tracking technology,** and it does not track you.

The Software produces **report files** (HTML) that you open in your web browser. These report files:

- are stored **locally** on your computer;
- contain **no** cookies, no tracking pixels, no analytics, and no scripts that phone home;
- do not load anything from the internet (audio and images are embedded or linked from your own local
  folder).

The only network activity the Software performs is the one-time speech-model download described in the
Privacy Policy, which does not set cookies on your machine.
